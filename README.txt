ART LIVE DASHBOARD — HOW TO PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE

1. Create a free GitHub account (if you don't have one).
2. Create a public or private repository, upload app.py and requirements.txt.
3. Open https://share.streamlit.io and sign in using GitHub.
4. Choose Create app, select the repository and app.py, then Deploy.
5. Open the generated Streamlit URL on your Samsung phone in Chrome.
6. In Chrome menu choose Add to Home screen.

Note: The free Streamlit service may sleep when idle and may change its pricing or limits. Quotes may be delayed; ART published performance is not a real-time account balance. The application does not require ART login credentials. Market ETF indicators are proxies, not the ART option unit prices. The app refreshes on page load with one-hour caching, not continuously. ART may change its website layout, causing table extraction to fail; official links remain available.
